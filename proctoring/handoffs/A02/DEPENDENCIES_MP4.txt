Reason: OpenCV wheels reliably write MJPG AVI but do not reliably provide an H.264

there is no network/downloader call in clips.py. Missing/unusable binary => logged AVI.

    $clipFfmpeg = & .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -c "import imageio_ffmpeg; print(imageio_ffmpeg.get_ffmpeg_exe())"
    & $clipFfmpeg -hide_banner -encoders | Select-String 'libx264'
